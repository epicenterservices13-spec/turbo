# Aquarium Tank Architectures & Engineering Specifications

> Structural load ratings, geometry formulas, glass thickness safety factors, and bioload containment standards.

---

## 📐 Tank Dimension & Capacity Calculations

$$\text{Gallons (US)} = \frac{L \times W \times H \text{ (inches)}}{231}$$
$$\text{Water Weight} \approx 8.34 \text{ lbs/gal } (1.0 \text{ kg/L})$$

Total load must include substrate (1.5–2 lbs/gal) and hardscape stone/timber displacement.

| **Up to 12" (30 cm)** | 4 mm - 6 mm | 3.5+ | Rimless / Floating base |
| **12" - 18" (45 cm)** | 6 mm - 8 mm | 3.2+ | Rimless / Euro-bracing |
| **18" - 24" (60 cm)** | 10 mm - 12 mm | 3.0+ | Euro-brace with center cross-brace |
| **24" - 36" (90 cm)** | 15 mm - 19 mm | 3.0+ | Heavy perimeter armor / Dual cross-braces |

## 🌿 Habitat Taxonomies

1. **Riparium / Aquatic Turtle Habitat (`/tanks/turtle_riparium.md`)**:
   - High filtration bioload margin (3x to 5x overrating).
   - Dedicated dry basking platform with targeted UVB/heat radiation gradients.
   - Submerged intake safety screens preventing shell/limb entrapment.

2. **High-Tech Planted Aquascape (`/tanks/planted_high_tech.md`)**:
   - Pressurized $CO_2$ injection with drop checker titration.
   - High PAR lighting arrays ($>50\ \mu\text{mol}/m^2/s$ at substrate).
   - Inert vs nutrient-loaded soil profiles (aquasoil, capped fluorite).

3. **Cichlid / High-Alkalinity Rockwork (`/tanks/african_cichlids.md`)**:
   - High-density buffering substrate (aragonite / crushed coral).
   - Structural eggcrate base protection under rock stacks.
