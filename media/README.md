# Filtration Media Specifications & Surface Chemistry

> Benchmarks for biological colonization surface areas (SSA), pore geometries, and chemical adsorptive media.

---

## 🧫 Biological Media: Specific Surface Area (SSA) Matrix

| Media Type | Structure | Usable SSA ($m^2/\text{liter}$) | Void Ratio (%) | Maintenance Life |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sintered Glass (e.g. Substrat Pro)** | Micro-porous fused beads | ~450 | ~50% | 3 - 5 Years |
| **Porous Ceramic Rings** | Open cylindrical extrusion | ~200 - 300 | ~65% | Indefinite (rinse only) |
| **Fluidized K1 / K3 Media** | HDPE buoyant biomedia | ~800 - 1000 | ~85% | Permanent (self-cleaning) |
| **Volcanic Pumice / Matrix** | Natural vesicular rock | ~300 - 400 | ~60% | Permanent |
| **Reticulated Polyurethane (20 PPI)** | Open-cell foam matrix | ~150 | ~90% | 2 - 4 Years |

---

## 🧵 Mechanical Media Porosity Standards

- **10–15 PPI (Pores Per Inch)**: Primary debris capture, coarse particulate exclusion.
- **20–30 PPI**: Secondary particulate trapping without premature clogging.
- **Fine Polishing Floss (< 50 micron)**: Water column clarification; disposable or bi-weekly replacement.

---

## 🧪 Chemical Adsorption & Ion Exchange

- **Activated Carbon**:
  - Base: Bituminous coal or acid-washed coconut shell.
  - Target: Phenols, tannins, heavy metals, medicinal residues.
  - Lifespan: 21 to 30 days before saturation/exhaustion.
- **Synthetic Macroporous Polymers (Purigen)**:
  - Selectively adsorbs soluble and insoluble organic compounds before nitrification conversion into ammonia/nitrite.
  - Regenerable with sodium hypochlorite (bleach) + dechlorination buffer.
- **Granular Ferric Oxide (GFO)**:
  - Binds reactive orthophosphates ($PO_4^{3-}$); critical for algae suppression.
