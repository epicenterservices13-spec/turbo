## ⚙️ Filter Classifications

### 1. Canister Filters
- Pressurized closed-loop circuits.
- Minimum turnover: **4x to 6x tank volume/hour** for general freshwater; **8x to 10x** for heavy bioload (turtles/monsters).
- Zero-bypass basket gasket verification.

### 2. Sump Filtration Systems
- Atmospheric open-loop reservoirs with gravity-fed weir overflows (BeanAnimal / Herbie silent drain configurations).
- Extreme biological chamber capacity (up to 30% of main display volume).
- Integrated equipment chambers (heaters, probes, ATO floats, reactors).

### 3. Sponge & Fluidized Moving Bed Arrays
- Air-lift powered biological filtration with high oxygenation efficiency.
- Ideal for fry rearing, quarantine, and secondary biological stabilization.
